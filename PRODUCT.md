# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers are AI engineers, researchers, and technically curious builders who want to understand dense notes on model mechanisms, long-context behavior, RAG, Agent systems, and application infrastructure. The author also uses the site as a personal research archive and as a portfolio-grade demonstration of AI application engineering.

## Product Purpose

This project is a personal AI systems research archive that also acts as an interactive knowledge product. It should help readers follow related ideas through public posts, understand technical arguments with less friction, and ask a grounded reading assistant about the author's published notes. Success means the public site feels authored, readable, technically credible, and supported by a reliable Blog Agent that cites public sources.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, precise. The product should feel like a maintained research notebook, not an AI-news site, SaaS landing page, or decorative portfolio template.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS visuals, excessive glass cards, star-field decoration as the main identity, theatrical copy, over-large hero typography, misleading feature claims, and chatbots that answer beyond available public sources. Public discovery must never expose hidden, draft, admin-only, or unavailable content.

## Design Principles

1. Reading comes before atmosphere: decoration can frame the archive, but article titles, paths, summaries, and source context must dominate.
2. Grounded before clever: AI answers must be source-backed, scoped, and honest when the public corpus is insufficient.
3. Structure is the product: series, research paths, tags, and citations should make the archive easier to traverse than a chronological blog.
4. Quiet precision beats spectacle: visual effects should feel intentional and restrained, not template-driven.
5. Owner tools serve publishing: admin and Agent operations should use familiar, task-focused product UI patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public reading and product surfaces. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast in dark mode, reduced-motion alternatives, mobile zoom, and resilient Chinese/English mixed typography.
